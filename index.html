Lightweight proactive anomaly detection for mobile robots

A small network watches a sliding window of onboard sensors, learns what normal driving looks like, and raises an alert when reconstruction, short-horizon prediction, and traversability disagree with that normal. The page collects the figures used for talks and for the paper supplement.
